## SAVAGE VISUAL — Rondo web — 2026-08-31

**Score: 24/100 → NOT SHIPPABLE**

Evidence: ~200 captures across 50+ routes × 320/390/768.

Start 100. Failed gates: G1 −8, G2 −10, G3 −8, G4 −8, G6 −10, G7 −10, G8 −6, G10 −6, G11 −10, G12 −8.

### KILL

1. **The product is empty.** Production guest Home, Map, Tournaments, Community, Scout, Reels, Messages, Notifications all have zero joinable matches. No priced card with slots exists. — `feed_empty_nav_covers_cta.png`, `map_empty_320.png`, `04-tournaments-390-fold.png` — fix: seed Metro Manila demo matches on production.

2. **White wordmark on cream is invisible.** `/rondo-logo.png` bakes white “RONDO” under the mark. Feed, login, signup, map, onboarding all lose the name. Landing then prints a second “Rondo” beside it. — `landing_dual_wordmark_three_ctas.png`, `login_too_many_doors_320.png`, `FeedHeader.tsx:14` — fix: icon-only asset + CSS wordmark in `--ink-hi`.

3. **Floating icon-only nav covers CTAs.** `fixed bottom-6`, tabs `h-11 w-[52px]`, labels are `aria-label` only. Covers Home “Around you” CTA and Profile “Messages”. — `feed_empty_nav_covers_cta.png`, `guest_profile_apps_wallet.png`, `BottomNav.tsx:137-155` — fix: labels + `pb` so pills never sit on buttons.

4. **Signup primary looks disabled.** “Get OTP” is `variant="secondary"` grey-on-cream. Facebook blue is the loudest thing on the screen. — `signup_get_otp_grey.png`, `app/(auth)/signup/page.tsx:163` — fix: `variant="primary"`.

5. **Scout / Reels are a different app.** Near-black full-bleed + white pill nav vs cream Matchday everywhere else. — `scout_dark_empty.png`, `13-reels-390-fold.png` — fix: one paper token; dark only inside the clip stage, not the chrome.

### FIX

6. **Login has too many doors.** Passkey + phone + email + two guest tiles + Apple/Google/Facebook. Hierarchy dies. — `login_too_many_doors_320.png`, `app/(auth)/login/page.tsx` — fix: one primary (phone OTP), social in a single overflow, guest as a text link.

7. **Landing stacks three competing actions.** Create account / Log in (ghost) / Continue as guest. — `landing_dual_wordmark_three_ctas.png`, `app/page.tsx:119-130` — fix: one gold CTA, login as text, guest quieter.

8. **Gold-on-cream links fail contrast.** “My matches”, “Street map”, Scout pill, “Manage”, filter “ALL · 0”. — `feed_empty_nav_covers_cta.png` — fix: `--gold` only on fills; links use `--ink-hi` + weight.

9. **OTP Continue is a ghost until digits exist.** Empty state uses `disabled:opacity-40` on the primary, so first glance looks broken. — `04-otp-390-fold.png`, `app/(auth)/otp/page.tsx:128` — fix: keep full contrast, disable pointer-events only.

10. **Map at 320 clips chips** (“Quez…”) and often still shows a skeleton pill. After load, production guest map does reach “No games on the map” + Browse feed (video 00:08). Fold captures frequently miss that. — `map_empty_320.png`, `guest_nav_tour_dead_ends.webm` — fix: wrap chips; don’t leave the skeleton up; one CTA is enough.

11. **404s are grey sentences.** “Match not found” / “Player not found” — no art, no back, no browse. Local missing-id often stuck on skeletons. — `match_not_found_dead_end.png`, `app/(player)/games/[id]/page.tsx:98-103`, `profile/[id]/page.tsx:294-298` — fix: EmptyState + Home CTA; timeout skeletons.

12. **Messages / Notifications empties have no forward action.** Italic “MESSAGES” / “NOTIFICATIONS”, Lucide bubble, copy only. — `messages_empty_no_cta.png`, `app/(player)/messages/page.tsx:51-58` — fix: one button to Community or Scout.

13. **Community double-asks Sign up** (banner card + empty-state card). Both `href="/signup"` with no `next=`. — `community_double_signup.png`, `community/page.tsx:268,291` — fix: one CTA; `href={/signup?next=/community}`.

14. **Guest profile is a fake player.** 0 Goals / 0 Cups / **0 Apps**, Wallet, Edit, Delete account. Nav covers Messages. — `guest_profile_apps_wallet.png`, `profile/[id]/page.tsx:398,535` — fix: guest shell (“You’re browsing. Create an account to keep matches.”); rename Apps → Games.

15. **Onboarding is a third visual system.** Role cards use italic uppercase on photos (`font-black italic uppercase`). Profile is a dark photographic sheet (“Make games fit you”) that contradicts cream Matchday. Copy says “three quick details”; the form asks name (again) + area + position + level. — `onboarding_role_italic.png`, `onboarding_profile_dark.png`, `onboarding/role/page.tsx:157`, `onboarding/profile/page.tsx:163-168` — fix: roman headings; same paper as the app; don’t re-ask name.

16. **Tournaments jargon.** “Matchday shelf” / “City bracket board” / “Run the table” over Live 0 / Open 0 / Total 0. — `04-tournaments-390-fold.png`, `tournaments/page.tsx:90` — fix: “Tournaments” + “No cups in your city yet”.

17. **Help “Submit Ticket” is washed gold.** Italic “HELP”. — `09-help-390-fold.png`, `10-help-new-390-fold.png`, `help/page.tsx:40` — fix: roman title; primary submit.

18. **`userScalable: false`.** `app/layout.tsx:31-32` — fix: allow pinch-zoom.

19. **No PWA manifest.** — fix: `manifest.webmanifest` + icons.

20. **Lucide + Phosphor mixed** across auth, my-games, organizer, messages, role `Check`. Organizer/admin use `emerald-300` / `sky-300` / hex `ALL_COLORS` / `text-red-400` instead of Matchday tokens. — `organizer/dashboard/page.tsx:30-36`, `organizer/create/match/page.tsx:20-28,91` — fix: Phosphor-only; tokenized status.

### POLISH

21. Next.js “N” / “1 Issue” overlay sits on the nav in local captures (dev-only).
22. `divide-white/5` leftover on messages list (`messages/page.tsx:60`).
23. Champion / bracket for missing ids still offer “Share champion” / “View bracket” with 0 wins (`34-…champion`, `35-…bracket`).
24. Auth pages stay a phone column at 768 (intentional for this shell; don’t stretch to a marketing desktop).

One good thing: empty Home/Help/Community at least have a gold button and a photo, unlike Map/404.

**J6 Recovery — ABANDONED.**

### KILL (flow)

1. Zero paths to a joinable match on live or local. Seed data.
2. Guest “Sign up” / Matches / Wallet often **forget intent** and land on empty Home. Treat `is_guest` the same as anonymous in `signup/page.tsx:33`. Preserve `next=`.
3. Icon-only nav: Matches vs Tournaments is unguessable; Home stays highlighted while Matches loads.

### FIX (flow)

4. Don’t mention Supabase in user-facing errors (`lib/auth/format-auth-error.ts:13`).
5. Don’t skip the OTP screen on fallback without saying “signed in with a backup method”.
6. Name is asked on signup and again on onboarding profile.
7. Organizer create: defer color hex / extra teams until after publish.
8. Time out feed/tournaments fetches; never leave skeletons forever.
9. Pinch-zoom + PWA.

## HALLMARK AUDIT — entire app

Genre declared in docs: night Matchday (`docs/rondo-design-system.md` still says **black ground, no box-shadows**). Shipped web is cream paper + nav shadow. Scout/Reels/onboarding-profile are night. Expo is `#0A0A0A`. **critical: design-system drift.**

### critical

- **Italic headers (38a)** — `onboarding/role/page.tsx:157`; `help/page.tsx:40`; `messages/page.tsx:41`; notifications header; scout empty “NO CLIPS YET”; shortlist “EMPTY SHORTLIST”. → roman, weight only.
- **Full-viewport photographic hero + three CTAs** — `app/page.tsx`. → one action.
- **Pure black / pure white split** — scout/reels `#000` vs cream app. → one paper.
- **Card-in-card** — Community guest banner + empty-state surface both with Sign up.
- **Mid-render token improvisation** — `text-red-400`, `emerald-300`, `sky-300`, `ALL_COLORS` hex in organizer create/dashboard.
- **Stamp vs page** — design system “Layer 0 is black”; pixels are cream. → update the law or the UI, not both.
- **Default-attractor sameness** — every player list is cream + gold pill + ALL-CAPS empty headline + soccer-ball icon.

### major

- **The AI nav (floating pill, genre-blind)** — icon-only, no labels, `BottomNav.tsx`. Expo tabs at least show emoji + label (`mobile/app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx`) while hiding the native label.
- **Interrogation onboarding** — name, phone, role, name, area, position, level before a single match.
- **Invented jargon** — Matchday shelf, City bracket board, Apps, payment_status chips.
- **Missing system reference on pages** — no Hallmark stamp; `design.md` absent (docs exist but pages don’t bind to them).

### minor

- Mixed Lucide/Phosphor.
- “Three quick details” vs four fields.
- `divide-white/5` on a light list.

**Summary — 7 critical · 4 major · 3 minor**
**Verdict — ships as slop on empty + contrast; the Matchday tokens are real, the product is not.**
